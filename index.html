My Favorite Anime

One Piece

One Piece is about Monkey D. Luffy and his crew of pirates exploring the Grand Line to find the greatest treasure ever, the One Piece.

Why Watch: It's an epic adventure with unforgettable characters, a rich world, and deep emotional moments.

Dr. Stone

Dr. Stone follows Senku Ishigami as he attempts to rebuild civilization after a mysterious event petrifies humanity.

Why Watch: It's a perfect blend of science, survival, and adventure, with clever problem-solving and unique characters.

Feriein: The Journey After the End

This anime follows Feriein, an elf mage with an eternal life, exploring the consequences of immortality on emotions and human relationships.

Why Watch: It's thought-provoking with a unique concept that blends fantasy and philosophical themes.

Horimiya

Horimiya is a romantic comedy about two high school students, Hori and Miyamura, who are very different at school but have a deep connection outside of it.

Why Watch: It's a sweet, relatable story about friendship, love, and self-discovery with amazing chemistry between characters (If you dont like it we are going to have a problem).

Assassination Classroom

Assassination Classroom centers on a class of misfit students who are tasked with killing their alien teacher, Koro-sensei, before he destroys Earth.

Why Watch: It's an emotional rollercoaster with humor, action, and a deep exploration of what makes a teacher great.
